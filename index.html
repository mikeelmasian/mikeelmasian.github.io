About me

I'm a product manager focused on growth and monetization in B2B software. My work has spanned Fidelity's trading platforms, Maxwell Health's benefits marketplace, Sana Benefits, and Clio's self-serve business. Most of it comes down to the same question: what makes someone start using a product, and what makes them pay for more of it.

My career began on software development teams at Fidelity, where I rebuilt the data and analytics platform behind one of the firm's largest trading platforms. I became the person the organization came to for user and behavioral data, which turned out to be a short path onto the product team. The first product I owned there was a freemium offering built to convert free users into paying customers. That's where the PLG part started.

I then moved to Maxwell Health, a benefits platform that Sun Life acquired in 2018, and later to Sana Benefits.

I now work on growth product at Clio, where I focus on self-serve motion design, activation, and monetization. The work sits at the intersection of product, pricing, and data, which is roughly where I've ended up every time.

Outside of that, I build tools and write about the parts of this job I think are underexplained: opportunity sizing, pricing and packaging, and what happens to product-led growth when the product itself is an AI model. I'm in Austin and originally from New England.
